## Teaching

My teaching mirrors my research: the future of work and technological change on the substantive side, hands-on quantitative methods on the methodological side. I also enjoy supervising and advising students, including in practice-oriented settings like the Integrative Project course. At the University of St. Gallen, I have taught at both the Bachelor's and Master's level.

### University of St. Gallen
Spring 2025 & 2026: Integrative Project, Bachelor Business Administration

Fall 2024: Introduction to Quantitative Methods, Bachelor International Affairs

Fall 2022: The Future of Work, Master International Affairs
